About

What is APOCAT?

APOCAT is a fixed-supply ERC-20 meme token created as a community-driven collectible on the Ethereum blockchain. It has no mint function, no ownership controls, and a permanently limited supply of 690,420 tokens, with over 50% sent to a burn address.

Why APOCAT?

APOCAT is just a cat in the apoCATlypse — a fun meme token for the community. While it serves primarily as a collectible digital asset and community token, our mission is simple: grow and find fellow survivors in the apoCATlypse.

What Makes It Special?

The token operates on Ethereum Mainnet as a standard ERC-20, ensuring compatibility with all major wallets and decentralised exchanges. Its fixed supply and burned tokens create a deflationary model, with no possibility of additional tokens ever being created.

No mint function

The contract contains no mint function. The supply of 690,420 is final and cannot be increased under any circumstance.

Burn is permanent

Over 50% of tokens are sent to 0x000...dead — a provably unspendable address, verifiable on Etherscan at any time.

No ownership controls

There is no owner, no admin key, and no pause function. The contract cannot be changed, upgraded, or halted by anyone.
